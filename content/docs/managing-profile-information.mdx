# Managing Profile Information

Your profile information is the base layer of your Nukleio portfolio data. It powers the parts of your portfolio that tell visitors who you are, what you do, where to find you, and how to contact you.

## Profile Fields

Depending on your account and dashboard configuration, your profile can include fields such as:

- email address
- phone number
- full name
- location or address
- current company
- current position
- bio
- GitHub URL
- LinkedIn URL
- X URL
- Facebook URL
- Instagram URL

You do not need to use every field. Add the information your portfolio actually displays, then return later when your design or content needs change.

## Writing a Useful Bio

Your bio should help visitors quickly understand who you are and what kind of work you do.

A strong portfolio bio usually includes:

- your main role or area of focus
- the technologies or types of products you enjoy building
- a short note about your experience, education, or interests
- enough personality to feel human without becoming too long

Example structure:

```txt
I am a software developer focused on full-stack web applications, developer tools, and clean user experiences. I enjoy building practical systems with React, Next.js, TypeScript, and cloud-backed APIs.
```

Keep the first sentence clear. Many portfolio designs use the bio in compact sections where long paragraphs can become hard to scan.

## Contact Details

Only add contact information that you are comfortable displaying or making available to a connected portfolio.

Before saving contact details, check that:

- your email address is correct
- your phone number is formatted consistently
- your location does not reveal more than you intend
- your portfolio design handles missing optional fields gracefully

If you do not want a contact detail shown publicly, leave it blank or remove it from the portfolio frontend.

## Social and Professional Links

Nukleio can store links to common professional and social profiles.

Recommended link practices:

- use full URLs, such as `https://github.com/username`
- open each link after saving to confirm it works
- avoid linking to inactive or incomplete profiles
- keep profile usernames consistent when possible

Useful links for a developer portfolio often include GitHub, LinkedIn, and a live resume or personal site.

## Updating Profile Information

To update your profile:

1. Open the profile or settings area in the Nukleio dashboard.
2. Review the existing values.
3. Switch into edit mode if needed.
4. Change the fields you want to update.
5. Save your changes.
6. Refresh your connected portfolio to confirm the update appears as expected.

If your portfolio does not show the new value immediately, check whether your portfolio caches API responses or requires a rebuild.

## Profile Checklist

Use this checklist before connecting a portfolio site:

- Name is spelled correctly.
- Bio is clear and concise.
- Current role and company are up to date.
- GitHub and LinkedIn links open correctly.
- Contact details are intentional and safe to share.
- Optional fields that you do not want displayed are blank or unused by the portfolio frontend.
