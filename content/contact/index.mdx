# Contact Nukleio

Have a question, found a problem, or need help with your account? Contact the Nukleio team and include enough information for us to understand the issue.

## Product Support

For help using Nukleio, contact:

**Email:** [nukleio.official@gmail.com](mailto:nukleio.official@gmail.com?subject=Nukleio%20Product%20Support)

Examples of product support requests include:

- trouble signing in
- problems updating profile or project information
- file or image upload errors
- API key questions
- portfolio integration problems
- unexpected dashboard or analytics behavior
- resume, cover letter, or headshot generation issues

When reporting a technical problem, include:

- the page or feature you were using
- what you expected to happen
- what actually happened
- any error message you received
- your browser and device type
- screenshots, when useful

Do not email passwords, API keys, payment card numbers, or other sensitive credentials.

## Billing Support

For subscription, payment, cancellation, or account-plan questions, contact:

**Email:** [nukleio.official@gmail.com](mailto:nukleio.official@gmail.com?subject=Nukleio%20Billing%20Support)

Include the email address associated with your Nukleio account. Do not send complete payment card details. Payments are processed through our payment provider.

## Privacy and Data Requests

For questions about personal information, account data, or privacy rights, contact:

**Email:** [nukleio.official@gmail.com](mailto:nukleio.official@gmail.com?subject=Nukleio%20Privacy%20Request)

You may contact us to request:

- access to personal information associated with your account
- correction of inaccurate information
- deletion of your account information
- information about how your data is used
- assistance with another privacy-related concern

We may need to verify your identity before completing a request.

See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for more information.

## Security Reports

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability, contact:

**Email:** [nukleio.official@gmail.com](mailto:nukleio.official@gmail.com?subject=Nukleio%20Security%20Report)

Please include:

- a description of the issue
- the affected page, endpoint, or feature
- steps needed to reproduce it
- the potential impact
- supporting screenshots or logs

Do not publicly disclose a vulnerability before we have had a reasonable opportunity to investigate it.

## Feedback and Feature Requests

Suggestions for new integrations, AI agents, dashboard features, or documentation improvements are welcome.

Send feedback to:

**Email:** [nukleio.official@gmail.com](mailto:nukleio.official@gmail.com?subject=Nukleio%20Feedback)

We may use submitted feedback to improve Nukleio without an obligation to compensate the person who provided it.

## Response Times

We aim to review messages promptly, but response times may vary depending on request volume, complexity, and availability.

For urgent security concerns, clearly include **Security Report** in the subject line and describe the potential impact.
